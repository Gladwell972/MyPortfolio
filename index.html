Hello, I’m Gladwell Sandile Sibiya.

I was born in Sibayeni, in the Nkomazi area of Mpumalanga, South Africa. My education journey began at Ingwenyama Primary School, where I studied from Grade R to Grade 8, before continuing at Mgubho Secondary School from Grade 9 to Grade 12. I completed my National Senior Certificate with a Bachelor Pass.

My interest in technology began when I was young. Around Grade 2, I became curious about computers and electronics and wanted to understand how they worked. By Grade 8, I had developed a lasting interest in DevOps and the systems behind software delivery.

I’m currently in my third year of a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at the University of the Free State, majoring in Computer Science and Business Management. I enjoy developing web applications, learning new tools and exploring how technology can solve real-world problems.
